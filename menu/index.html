* Served raw or undercooked. Consuming raw or undercooked meats, poultry, seafood, shellfish, or eggs may increase your risk of foodborne illness.

Our kitchen handles fish, shellfish, molluscs, soy, sesame, wheat and egg, so we cannot promise that any dish is without them, even those described as vegan or vegetarian. Please tell us about any allergy before you order.

Soy sauce contains wheat and soy. Tamari is available on request, for example for a guest who avoids wheat. Check the label of the tamari you buy: some contain wheat.

Molluscs (squid, octopus, scallop, clam) are listed as their own word. They are not one of the nine major allergens named in US law; we list them because guests ask. Coconut is also listed on its own, not as a tree nut.
